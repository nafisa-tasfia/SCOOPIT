HOW SCOOPIT WORKS

Verification Methodology

Our Fact-Checking Process

  1. Claim Identification: Extract factual statements from selected news articles.
  2. Source Discovery: Identify relevant official, institutional, and independent sources.
  3. Evidence Collection: Collect supporting, contradicting, and contextual evidence.
  4. Source Assessment: Evaluate publisher authority, authenticity, relevance, transparency, and recency.
  5. Human Verification: Examine evidence and assign a final verdict.
  6. Documentation: Record findings, limitations, and evidence gaps.

Verdict Categories

  • Verified: Supported by reliable evidence.
  • Partially True: Some parts are supported, while others need qualification.
  • Misleading: The statement creates an inaccurate impression due to framing or missing context.
  • False: Reliable evidence contradicts the claim.
  • Unverifiable: Sufficient evidence is unavailable to reach a conclusion.
